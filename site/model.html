How the model works

Versioned football forecasts built from information available before kickoff.

Team context

Lagged offence/defence, opponent matchups, rest and venue feed continuous expected scoring. QB source state is explicit; no rejected replacement-value experiment is active.

Player opportunity

Lagged carries, targets, current-team volume and red-zone/goal-line history feed the registered player and TD models. Eligibility follows frozen roster, injury and transaction checks.

Simulation

10,000 correlated simulations produce the existing win, spread and total distributions. The headline score remains the independently rounded expected score, not a certified exact-score mode.

Point-in-time discipline

Each prediction references an immutable lock and precedes kickoff. Current-week outcomes are never inputs to that week's pregame model.

Bookmaker prices do not enter the prediction model

Fair decimal odds are the reciprocal of model probability. Bookmaker comparisons are downstream, separately timestamped snapshots. Paired prices allow margin removal; exchange commission is not invented where unprovided.

What confidence means

Forecast confidence is distinct from value confidence, which also considers quote freshness, data completeness and role certainty.

Current limitations

Unknown injury information does not mean healthy. Depth-based expected starters are not game-day confirmations. Player yardage ranges retain the incumbent heuristic, not an empirically certified distribution. Unsupported passing, reception, fantasy and multi-TD markets remain unavailable as production forecasts.
