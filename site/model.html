How the model works

A registered, versioned forecast engine built around information genuinely known before kickoff.

Model updated —

01

Team context

Recent offence and defence efficiency, opponent matchups, rest, venue and quarterback context shape expected scoring.

02

Player opportunity

Lagged carries, targets, red-zone work and active-roster context estimate meaningful rushing and receiving roles.

03

Simulation

10,000 correlated simulations turn continuous score forecasts into win probabilities and honest uncertainty ranges.

04

Point-in-time discipline

Every run references an immutable lock. Current-week outcomes and post-kickoff information are rejected.

CORE PRINCIPLE

Bookmaker odds do not enter the prediction model.

“Model Fair Odds” are simply the reciprocal of the model probability. Market prices are loaded only after the independent prediction is frozen.

What confidence means

Model confidence describes the independent forecast. Value confidence separately considers market freshness, data completeness and player-role certainty without changing the model probability.

Current limitations

Market comparisons require timestamped bookmaker quotes and exact fixture or player mappings. Unsupported or unavailable markets remain blank rather than being reconstructed from untimestamped schedule fields.
